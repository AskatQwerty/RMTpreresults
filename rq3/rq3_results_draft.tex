\subsection{RQ3: Consistency and Stability of SHAP Explanations}
\label{subsec:rq3}
\paragraph{Set-up.}
The preliminary RQ3 experiment uses the Level-4 pilot models of Section~\ref{subsec:rq1}, with 322 raw features and 755 transformed columns. One fixed list contains 1,000 locked-test applicants, 500 with and 500 without bureau records (seed 42; default rate 8.6\% in each group). The common background contains 100 pilot-training applicants. Saved base fits explain all 1,000 applicants; ensembles explain the first 150 per group. Five retrainings per model use saved best hyperparameters and stratified 90\% subsamples of the 20,000 training applicants, seeds 1--5. Retraining explanations use 500 applicants for base models and 300 for ensembles, always from the same fixed list.

All explanations concern default probability. Interventional Tree SHAP~\cite{lundberg2020trees} explains random forest, XGBoost and LightGBM; transformed contributions are summed to raw features. LR, MLP and ensembles use forward/reverse permutation Shapley estimates: two permutation pairs for saved LR/MLP fits, one for ensembles and retrainings. This replaces the planned LR log-odds explainer to maintain a common probability scale. Grouped raw-feature masking and aggregated transformed-column Tree SHAP remain different explanation constructions, which can affect cross-model agreement.

\paragraph{Agreement with LR.}
Table~\ref{tab:rq3_agree} separates applicant-level and global agreement on saved fits. Tree models and MLP share 0.28--0.37 of an applicant's top-five reasons with LR; soft voting shares 0.54 and stacking 0.68. Their global ranking correlations with LR are 0.54--0.58 for trees, 0.66 for MLP, 0.81 for voting and 0.87 for stacking. XGBoost and LightGBM agree more closely with each other (global Spearman 0.93; top-five overlap 0.73). Random forest's overlap with LR is 0.32 with bureau history and 0.24 without it; corresponding values are 0.37/0.36 for XGBoost and 0.36/0.33 for LightGBM. These are descriptive subgroup comparisons.

\paragraph{Stability after retraining.}
Table~\ref{tab:rq3_stability} averages ten run pairs. Its supplied caption gives the base-model sample size; ensembles use 300 applicants. All applicant-level criteria concern overlap averaged over pairs. Global correlations exceed the pre-set 0.8 criterion for every model except MLP (0.79); they range from 0.89 to 0.97 for the others. Only random forest meets the mean four-of-five criterion: top-five overlap is 0.81, compared with LR 0.78, voting 0.77, LightGBM and stacking 0.74, XGBoost 0.69 and MLP 0.30. Paired Bonferroni-adjusted intervals exclude zero for RF's positive difference and the negative differences of XGBoost, LightGBM, MLP and stacking; voting's interval includes zero. Thus the observed direction of H3 holds for the latter four models, does not hold for voting, and is reversed for RF.

Mean within-applicant standard deviations of predicted probability are 0.007--0.015, except MLP (0.080). Full-test refit metrics, reported in the repository, have PR-AUC standard deviations of 0.0009--0.0020; the largest ROC-AUC standard deviation is 0.0037 for MLP. These measure retraining variability separately from the saved-fit comparisons. Global stability for MLP is 0.80 with bureau history and 0.74 without it. Other models exceed the global criterion in both groups.

\paragraph{Explainer noise and errors.}
Independent explanations of the same saved fit, using one permutation pair, retain 0.90 [0.89, 0.91] of LR's top-five reasons ($n=500$), about 0.88 [0.86, 0.89] for voting and 0.77 [0.75, 0.80] for stacking ($n=150$ for ensembles). MLP retains 0.39 [0.37, 0.41]. Stacking's retraining overlap of 0.74 is therefore close to its same-fit noise reference. This is a conditional diagnostic, not an exact decomposition of instability into explanation noise and retraining. Tree explainers are deterministic.

On the saved MLP fit, 38.9\% of explained probabilities are below $10^{-6}$ and 0.5\% above $1-10^{-6}$. Its median sum of absolute contributions is 1.05, versus 0.59 for LR and 0.14--0.20 for trees. MLP remains in the comparisons. Across run pairs, the shares of applicants averaging fewer than three retained reasons are 8.2\% for LR, 2.2\% RF, 19.6\% XGBoost, 5.6\% LightGBM, 5.3\% voting, 9.0\% stacking and 98.8\% MLP. Saved-fit additivity residuals are below $8\times10^{-5}$; available refit diagnostics reach 0.001694 for LightGBM and 0.001339 for RF. Raw outputs are retained. Bootstrap intervals use 1,000 applicant resamples, seed 42, conditional on the fitted models, selected applicants, background and realized explanations; they do not establish population-wide stability.

\subsection{Reproducibility}
Code and complete aggregate outputs are at \url{https://github.com/AskatQwerty/RMTpreresults}, in \texttt{rq3/}. Pilot training used Windows 11, Ryzen 7 7435HS, 16~GB RAM, Python 3.13.9, scikit-learn 1.7.2, XGBoost 3.2.0 and LightGBM 4.7.0; search and refits took 10,640~s. Saved explanations and seeds 1--4 used a two-core Xeon Linux container, 7~GB RAM, Python 3.13.17 and SHAP 0.54.1. Seed 5 used Windows, Core i5-10300H, 8~GB RAM and Python 3.13.1, with identical pinned research libraries, applicant IDs and background. Seeds are 42 for selection/search/bootstrap, 1--5 for retraining, and 0, 11, 12 for permutation explanations. Saved-fit explanations took about 35 minutes. Detailed timings and numerical diagnostics accompany the repository; applicant data and fitted models remain in the private handoff.

RQ3: SHAP agreement and stability for the November models
Repository: AskatQwerty/RMTpreresults, commit f431692a194381c1e5fd28af4235a7597dcc37a1.

Seven Level 4 models were evaluated. Each applicant's predicted default probability is explained using 322 original features.
All seven models, including both ensembles, use the same 1,000 test applicants: 500 with a bureau record and 500 without one. The background comprises 100 training applicants.
Each model was retrained five times with seeds 1–5 on stratified samples of 18,000 applicants. Stability was compared over ten pairs of runs on the same 1,000 applicants for all seven models.
Tree models use interventional Tree SHAP. Logistic regression, MLP and ensembles use forward and reverse permutations of original features. All explanations use the same P(TARGET=1) scale.

There are 50,000 explanation rows for 1,000 unique applicants. Five original explanation sets already covered all 1,000 applicants and were verified and reused. The other 45 were recalculated. The same 35 saved retrained models were used for the five repeats.
The October stability summary used 500 applicants for individual models and 300 for ensembles, so its samples differ from November. The direct comparison of original October and November MLPs uses the same 1,000 applicants.

Global correlation ranks features by mean absolute SHAP. Personal top-5 overlap is averaged across applicants. The project's stability thresholds are 0.80 for both measures.
Confidence intervals use 1,000 bootstrap samples of applicants with seed 42. Paired comparisons against logistic regression use a Bonferroni correction for six comparisons.

October-to-November MLP change
On the same 1,000 applicants, global feature-importance correlation is 0.606 and mean personal top-5 overlap is 37.0%. The original October explanations come from the saved fixed run of the five-configuration pilot.

Checks and limitations
All seven original final Pipelines were loaded from the official RMT_active_28_models.zip bundle. The archive and every model were checked by SHA-256. SHAP values and the noise control were computed directly on the published models. Across 61,503 test applicants, the largest absolute difference from published AP, ROC-AUC, log loss and Brier was 1.55e-08. The comparison used aggregate metrics.
MLP follows the November training procedure: internal validation selects epochs, followed by a fresh fit. The final fit selected 21 epochs.
Versions, inputs, limitations and local execution details are recorded in verification/. Random forest used two threads via ThreadPoolExecutor. LR and ensemble permutations used accelerated computation of the same contributions, checked against predict_proba on masked examples.
The maximum difference between the sum of SHAP plus its base value and the model probability is 0.00582159; six rows exceed 0.001. See verification/explanation_checks.csv.
For four approximate explainers, personal top-5 reproducibility was evaluated on the same fitted model using permutation seeds 11 and 12. Results are in tables/rq3_explainer_noise.csv. Conclusions apply to this pilot, the selected applicants and the chosen background.

Archive layout

selection/ — fixed explained-applicant IDs, background and feature order. Each SHAP column corresponds to its position in features_level_4.json.
verification/ — checks, parameter provenance, versions, checksums and reproduction materials.
